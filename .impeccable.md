## Design Context

### Users
Hospital chain clinical trial coordinators and researchers. They use this dashboard as an initial patient screening layer — shortlisting candidates for clinical trials. The workflow sits between a quick check and deep analysis: significantly faster than manual review, but not instant. Users are medical professionals who value precision, clarity, and trust in the data.

### Brand Personality
Clinical, modern, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a precision medical instrument — confident, quiet authority. Not flashy, not cold. Think: the calm professionalism of a well-designed diagnostic tool.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Dark — users may work extended sessions, clinical environments often have mixed lighting
- **Tone**: Institutional precision with warmth. Dense information presented with clear hierarchy
- **Palette**: Deep navy base tinted toward teal. Muted teal accent for primary actions. Warm amber for key metrics. Coral for exclusions/warnings. No AI-cyan, no purple gradients
- **Typography**: Clean sans-serif with high legibility. Clinical without being sterile
- **Anti-references**: Generic SaaS dashboards, gradient-heavy AI tools, glassmorphism

### Design Principles
1. **Density with clarity** — Show more data, not more decoration. Every pixel earns its place
2. **Quiet confidence** — The interface should feel authoritative without shouting. Restrained color, strong typography
3. **Trust through transparency** — Show the reasoning (criteria used, LLM extraction, score breakdowns) without overwhelming
4. **Instrument, not toy** — This aids medical decisions. Treat it with appropriate gravity
